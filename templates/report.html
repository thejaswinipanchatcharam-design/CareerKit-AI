1. Problem Statement & Research Objectives

Addressing generative hallucinations in employment applications.

Commercial AI writing tools frequently generate unverifiable claims, invent past employers, exaggerate metrics, or extrapolate unstudied technical skills to "beat" automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). This creates serious ethical hazards, employer distrust, and legal liabilities for job applicants.

Core Engineering Objectives:

  • Factual Grounding: Restrict AI synthesis strictly to candidate-verified profile data.
  • Candidate Sovereignty: Ensure candidates retain final editing, review, and approval authority over all generated content.
  • Audit Transparency: Automatically extract and categorize factual claims into an explainable 4-tier audit report.
  • Privacy Safeguards: Require explicit consent before AI processing and isolate private data from runtime logs.
  • Honest Metrics: Refuse fake "ATS percentage scores" in favor of transparent keyword alignment heuristics.

3. Factuality-Locked Prompt Engineering & Claim Auditing

Dual-layer guardrails combining low-temperature model conditioning and deterministic post-generation validation.

Layer 1: Temperature & Prompt Grounding

Generative models are conditioned with a strict temperature limit (T = 0.2), curtailing probabilistic token divergence. Prompts partition input data into explicit CANDIDATE FACTS (GROUND TRUTH) and TARGET JOB CONTEXT (REFERENCE BENCHMARK).

# SYSTEM INSTRUCTION DIRECTIVE:
1. NEVER invent employers, certifications, or metrics.
2. NEVER turn job requirements into candidate skills without ground-truth evidence.
3. Flag missing information as [NEEDS CANDIDATE INPUT].
4. Focus exclusively on reorganizing genuine achievements.

Layer 2: Deterministic 4-Tier Claim Audit

Following generation, regular-expression parsers scan the output document for factual assertions (degrees, tools, metrics, dates) and cross-reference them against the candidate's SQLite profile record:

✓ Supported by supplied info: Claim explicitly matches ground-truth profile records.
⚠️ Needs candidate confirmation: Numerical metrics or project scope requiring verification.
🔍 Unsupported: Skills or tools asserted that do not exist in candidate facts.
⛔ Contradiction detected: Degree or qualification conflicting with actual academic status.

5. Ethical Reflection & College Viva Defense Guide

Key distinctions, known limitations, and answers to critical technical review questions.

Q: Why doesn't CareerKit AI guarantee 100% ATS pass rates?

Answer: Commercial ATS vendors use proprietary, confidential algorithms. Any product claiming an "ATS guaranteed score" is engaging in deceptive marketing. CareerKit AI provides honest, explainable keyword matching and readability metrics rather than unscientific guarantees.

Q: How does the application prevent data leakage?

Answer: API keys are kept strictly in server-side .env configurations. Application logs record only metadata (e.g. latency, token length) without dumping raw private candidate text. Mandatory consent is required prior to external API transmission.

Q: What are the current system limitations?

Answer: Image-only scanned PDFs require external optical character recognition (OCR) and are flagged for manual text paste. Claim verification utilizes deterministic regular expressions and keyword taxonomies, which may not catch nuanced subjective claims.
